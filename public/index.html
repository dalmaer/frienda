THE PLEDGEF·N·D·A / 001

A solemn-ish promise

We, Juan and Kathy Batlle, Ivan and Amanda Dufresne Lee, and Dion and Emily Almaer, do hereby declare that anything said, shown, confessed, screenshotted, or dramatically whispered to anyone in this group becomes an instant FrieNDA moment.

It stays within this circle. No forwarding, quoting at brunch, “hypothetically” retelling it to another friend, or laundering it through a different group chat. A story does not become public domain just because it was funny.

We will protect each other’s secrets with the seriousness of a tiny dragon guarding one (1) emotionally important sandwich. If we slip, we will own it, tell the group, apologize, and help contain the spill.

If we knowingly break this pledge, we owe the circle snacks, a sincere apology, and one week wearing the ceremonial Hat of Mild Disappointment. This is a blood oath in the theatrical sense only. No blood, needles, curses, lawyers, or actual hats are required (the snacks are negotiable but strongly encouraged).

By signing, we promise good judgment and better group-chat manners.✦   NO ACTUAL BLOOD WAS DRAWN   ✦
